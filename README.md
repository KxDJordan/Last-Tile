# LAST TILE

A mobile-first battle royale tactics card game. Eight pieces fight on a floating 12×12 stone arena, and the board rots from the outside in. Each turn you walk and play cards from your 16-card deck, and the last piece standing wins.

**Play:** <https://kxdjordan.github.io/Last-Tile/>

The whole game is one self-contained HTML file (`index.html`). It loads only Three.js r128 from cdnjs and Google Fonts. Play it in portrait on a phone; it also works on desktop.

## How to play

- **Loadout:** pick a class (swipe or use the arrows) and one of its 3 pieces. Spend 10 stat points, choose a Gamebreaker and build a 16-card deck. Save loadouts in 4 preset slots.
- **Your turn:** walk up to your SPD in tiles (a step up costs 2) and play any cards you can afford with Energy, in any order and in as many separate walks as you like. Tap End Turn when you're done.
- **Cards:** tap a card and the tiles you can target light up. Tap a tile to preview the area, then tap it again (or press Confirm) to play the card.
- **Gamebreaker:** fill the meter by dealing and taking damage, opening caches and surviving ring closes. Using it costs no Energy and doesn't end your turn.
- **Camera:** drag to rotate, drag up or down to tilt, and pinch to zoom.

## Classes

| Class | Pieces | Passive |
|---|---|---|
| Vanguard | Bastion, Colossus, Warden | At the start of your turn, your shield becomes at least your DEF |
| Striker | Brawler, Duelist, Tempest | Your first Damage card each turn deals +2 if you moved this turn |
| Marksman | Ranger, Gunslinger, Artillerist | +1 range for each level you stand above the target |
| Phantom | Shade, Specter, Veilblade | The first hit each round deals half damage if you moved 3+ tiles last turn |
| Lifebinder | Verdant, Seraph, Blood Oracle | Heal 1 whenever you deal damage |
| Hexer | Rotcaller, Witchlight, Void Mask | Take half corruption damage |

## Source layout

`dist/last-tile.html` (published as `index.html`) is generated by `node build.js` from these modules:

| File | Module | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `src/data.js` | DATA | Classes, pieces, 56 cards, 18 Gamebreakers, deck rules |
| `src/core.js` | CORE | `Game` class with all the rules. It never touches the DOM or THREE; it calls a Promise-based view interface. |
| `src/ai.js` | AI | Bot controllers, also DOM-free |
| `src/view.js` | VIEW | Three.js scene, pieces, VFX, camera |
| `src/ui.js`, `src/main.js`, `src/shell.html`, `src/style.css` | UI | Loadout screen, HUD, targeting, end screen |

`CONTRACT.md` describes the interfaces between the modules, and `BRIEF.md` is the original design brief.

## Design decisions

- **Free movement (changed after v1.0).** There is no Move/Act stance. Each turn you get a walking budget equal to your effective SPD (no cap). You can walk before, between or after playing cards, in as many separate walks as you like, until the budget runs out. A flat step costs 1 and a step up 1 level costs 2. Walking never draws a card or ends your turn, and a root sets the budget to 0. Movement cards are extra movement on top of the budget.
- **Flow** ("moved this turn") is now easy to get, so the Striker passive is back to the brief's +2. The Phantom passive now needs 3+ tiles moved during your last turn.
- **Sudden death (not in the brief).** Three rounds after the last ring closes (round 19), every tile corrupts, the centre included. Corruption damage then rises by +3 every round. During sudden death, healing is halved, Cleanse and Sanctuary are disabled, and the Hexer's corruption protections stop working. Without this, cleansed tiles became permanent safe zones and endgames stalled.
- **Shields reset to 0 at the start of their owner's turn.** The Vanguard passive then raises the shield to DEF. Stacking shields caused unkillable stalemates.
- **"Within N"** uses Manhattan distance. Pillars block line of sight, but elevation doesn't. The cone is 1 tile, then the 3 tiles at distance 2.
- **Traps and caches** trigger only where a move ends, whatever moved the piece (walk, dash, teleport, push, pull or swap). A full hand stops drawing at 7 instead of burning cards.
- **Cache choice:** two options of 2 random cards (from your class and general pool), plus one Stat Shard, where you pick the stat.
- **Status durations** count down at the start of the affected player's turn. Root covers the target's next turn, Sap covers 2 of its turns, and Vanish lasts until your next turn.
- **Mirror Legion** places its 2 decoys automatically on random empty tiles within 2. Decoys block movement and vanish on any damage or debuff.
- **Eagle's Perch** puts your tile at height 3 and locks it. When the effect ends, the tile drops back to its original height.
- **Bots** use seeded randomness, play differently by class (each has its own ideal range, aggression, and healing and retreat weights), never see invisible players or enemy traps, and take at most 12 actions per turn. A 60-round cap exists as a safety net but was never reached in testing.

Full detail is in `test/SYSTEMS_NOTES.md`, `test/GRAPHICS_NOTES.md` and `test/UI_NOTES.md`.

With the brief's numbers unchanged, Striker won 3.3% of matches and Lifebinder 30%. Switching to free movement with the v1.0 numbers gave Vanguard 28.3%, Phantom 21.3% and Hexer 9.2%. That led to the new Phantom condition and the movement and ranged-card changes marked above. Full detail and win rates are in `test/SYSTEMS_NOTES.md`.
